Pigeon® 2.0 Mount Calibration
=============================

Mount Calibration can be performed once the Pigeon® 2.0 has been mounted to the robot. This will calculate the optimal offsets to apply to ensure that Pose, Pitch and Yaw is 0 when the robot is considered "flat". Users can access the Mount Calibration menu by clicking on the Pigeon® 2.0 in :guilabel:`Devices` and clicking :guilabel:`Calibration` in the top right.

Read through the on-screen instructions and click :guilabel:`Begin Mount Calibration`.
